# Frontier campaign update

Open **last-bastion-world-lab.html**. Save your current session before refreshing. Start a new game for the enclosed starting courtyard, spaced arrow towers and generated monster settlements. Existing saves retain their world and residents; unspent talent points are available for their character levels.

## Building

Press **B**. Choose a category and blueprint, point at the terrain, then **left-click or press F**. **Escape** leaves build mode without changing the camera.

- **Structure:** Walls, courtyard gates and ramps. Walls and gates occupy tile edges, leaving the interior available for towers. Aim at each desired side to build multiple edges.
- **Defenses:** Towers, traps and barbed wire. Wire has **Ground / On wall / In front** placement options. The latter two require a wall or gate on the same edge.
- **Ramps:** Choose a lower tile adjacent to terrain one level higher; **R** rotates the high end. Finished ramps become part of the terrain and navigation surface.
- Structures consume gold/materials and become construction plans. Laborers travel there to work. You can help from within 4m by repeatedly clicking the plan, pressing **F**, or using its interaction menu's work button. Each tap contributes work; holding a key does not auto-build.
- Courtyard gates open for nearby friendlies. Monsters attack closed gates and blocking defenses. Wire slows and damages nearby monsters.

## Residents and settlements

Use **E** beside an NPC, or aim at them in first person. Order them to follow, patrol their home portal, guard their current position, or guard a clicked position/object. Choose a pursuit radius; guards return when the target leaves it.

Laborer duty checkboxes enable **building, mining, wood collecting and combat**. Each enabled duty has High / Normal / Low priority. Laborers with low health retreat toward a friendly courtyard and recover near its portal.

Training buildings offer a list of eligible laborers, including followers and those carrying resources. A trainee delivers carried resources, travels to the building and trains for 30 seconds. Production buildings require an assigned laborer with the relevant gathering duty enabled.

The **Settlement** button browses residents and buildings for a selected portal. Issuing object-specific orders still requires being nearby.

## Portal defense and exploration

Use **E at a new friendly portal** to start each defense wave. Attacks arrive from around the claim. Each wave also creates a hostile portal inside the contested area, with red grid-based approach lines. These portals have health and continue spawning reinforcements between waves. The final wave cannot finish until all of that claim's hostile portals and remaining attackers are defeated.

Friendly portals have health, an immediate 6m spawn ward and magical defenses. Winning the defense suppresses spawning throughout the claim. Losing a gate removes its protection. Use a living gate's interaction menu to **teleport to another friendly gate** or transfer residents.

The wilderness supports more monsters, including **Dusk Stalkers** (rush), **Venom Spitters** (ranged poison), **Ironhide Bulwarks** (armor), **Cinder Bombers** (explosions) and **Rime Wraiths** (ranged slow). Monsters wander, engage visible friendlies and attack portals when entering their territory.

Hostile settlements contain strong portal defenders and destructible structures. Destroy their portal and mobile defenders, then use **E near the settlement's former portal** to capture surviving buildings. Destroyed buildings are not replaced.

Traveling merchants with mercenary escorts occasionally visit friendly gates. Look for a **gold banner** and use **E** to buy or sell resources and portal kits. Prices and available stock appear in the menu.

## Character progression

Start with your class's basic move. Levels grant talent points; **N / Talents** opens two branching paths per class. Higher choices require the previous choice in that branch and the displayed level. Moves remain locked until learned. The final passive differs by class: Knight health, Ranger cooldown recovery, or Pyromancer spell damage.

**1–6** selects a move; repeat the key or left-click to use it. Right-click an action-bar move to toggle automatic use. **Tab** cycles targets. **V** cycles cameras. Third person keeps the character centered and adjusts for obstructions.
